# RIGOR Treasury & Market Neutrality Policy

**Status:** Public Policy
**Applies to:** RIGOR Mainnet
**Effective:** RIGOR Mainnet Genesis
**Policy Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the principles and operational constraints governing the use of funds and assets controlled by the RIGOR project and the conduct of the RIGOR project team with respect to the market for RIGOR-native assets.

The purpose of this policy is to protect the long-term integrity, neutrality, transparency, and decentralization of the RIGOR network.

RIGOR is intended to be an open proof-of-work network.

The project team builds and maintains the protocol and its public infrastructure.

The project team does **not** determine the market value of ORU or RES.

Market participants determine market prices through voluntary and independent activity.

This policy is therefore designed to establish a clear separation between:

* protocol development;
* public infrastructure;
* treasury management;
* mining and network security;
* ecosystem development; and
* market price formation.

---

# 2. Core Principle

The RIGOR project follows one central principle:

> **Build the protocol. Build the infrastructure. Do not control the market.**

The project team may actively develop, maintain, secure, document, and promote the RIGOR network.

The project team will not make maintaining or increasing the market price of ORU or RES an operational objective.

The existence of a market price does not create a project obligation to support, stabilize, increase, or defend that price.

---

# 3. No Price Target

The RIGOR project does not establish:

* a target price for ORU;
* a target price for RES;
* a minimum market price;
* a maximum market price;
* a guaranteed valuation;
* a guaranteed return;
* a guaranteed liquidity level; or
* a guaranteed investment outcome.

Statements concerning the technical development, network activity, adoption, releases, infrastructure, mining, or future development of RIGOR should not be interpreted as promises that the market price of ORU or RES will increase.

The project team will not make statements whose primary purpose is to create a false or misleading expectation of future market value.

---

# 4. No Treasury Price Support

Treasury funds and assets will not be used for the purpose of artificially supporting, defending, or maintaining the market price of ORU or RES.

In particular, the project will not establish a standing program whose purpose is to:

* buy ORU solely to prevent a price decline;
* buy ORU solely to create artificial demand;
* sell ORU solely to manipulate a target price;
* maintain a predetermined price floor;
* create artificial scarcity for price support; or
* coordinate purchases or sales for the purpose of creating a misleading market signal.

The project may, where appropriate and lawful, use treasury resources for genuine operational purposes described in this policy.

Operational spending must not be disguised as market-price support.

---

# 5. No Pump-and-Dump Coordination

The RIGOR project will not knowingly participate in or coordinate a pump-and-dump scheme.

The project will not:

* coordinate artificial buying campaigns;
* instruct market participants to buy ORU or RES in order to create a price spike;
* manufacture artificial urgency around a market purchase;
* publish knowingly false or misleading information to increase demand;
* coordinate project-controlled accounts to create misleading market activity;
* arrange artificial trading volume;
* engage in wash trading;
* create false impressions of market demand or liquidity; or
* coordinate a promotional campaign whose stated or unstated purpose is to create an exit opportunity for project-controlled or affiliated holdings.

The RIGOR project will not ask its community to purchase ORU or RES merely to support the market price.

Public communications should focus on verifiable facts concerning the network, technology, development, infrastructure, mining, and ecosystem.

---

# 6. Market Neutrality

RIGOR does not require market participants to hold ORU or RES.

Miners may sell their block rewards.

Users may buy or sell ORU or RES.

Developers may hold or sell assets received for legitimate work.

Independent investors may enter or leave the market.

Exchanges may list or delist RIGOR assets according to their own policies.

Pools may operate independently.

The project team will not attempt to control these independent decisions for the purpose of influencing market price.

A healthy open network requires participants to be able to make independent economic decisions.

---

# 7. Exchange Listings

The RIGOR project may communicate with exchanges and other market infrastructure providers regarding technical integration, wallet support, deposits, withdrawals, blockchain compatibility, and other legitimate operational matters.

The project may also provide accurate technical and public information to exchanges conducting their own due diligence.

An exchange listing is considered infrastructure and market access, not a guarantee of price appreciation.

The RIGOR project will not represent an exchange listing as evidence that ORU or RES will increase in value.

The project will not make a listing dependent upon a commitment to manipulate market price or trading volume.

---

# 8. Independent Market Participants

RIGOR does not prohibit independent parties from purchasing, holding, selling, trading, mining, providing liquidity for, or otherwise participating in the market for ORU or RES, subject to applicable law and the rules of the relevant platform.

The project team does not control independent market participants.

An investor may independently purchase a substantial amount of ORU.

A miner may independently accumulate or sell ORU.

A third-party exchange may independently list ORU.

A third-party market maker may independently provide liquidity.

Such independent activity does not constitute an endorsement, guarantee, or commitment by the RIGOR project.

The project team will not provide preferential treatment to a market participant in exchange for that participant attempting to influence the price of ORU or RES.

---

# 9. Investor Relationships

The RIGOR project may communicate with individuals, companies, funds, miners, exchanges, developers, infrastructure providers, and other organizations interested in participating in the ecosystem.

Potential investors or strategic partners may contribute capital, equipment, development resources, infrastructure, expertise, or other resources to RIGOR.

However, the project will not accept an arrangement under which an investor's participation is conditioned upon the project team:

* maintaining a specific token price;
* guaranteeing a return;
* conducting buybacks for the investor's benefit;
* creating artificial market demand;
* suppressing legitimate selling activity;
* coordinating a pump;
* providing non-public information for trading purposes; or
* otherwise manipulating the market.

The project may cooperate with legitimate investors who independently choose to acquire ORU or participate in the RIGOR ecosystem.

The project does not guarantee those investors a profit.

---

# 10. Information Neutrality

The project team will seek to disclose material public information consistently and transparently.

The project team will not intentionally provide selected market participants with confidential project information for the purpose of allowing them to trade ahead of a public announcement.

Where practical, significant project announcements should be made through public channels.

Examples include:

* major protocol releases;
* consensus changes;
* security disclosures;
* official infrastructure changes;
* wallet releases;
* mining software releases;
* major exchange integrations;
* changes to treasury policy; and
* material changes to the project's development direction.

This principle is intended to reduce information asymmetry between the project team and the broader RIGOR community.

---

# 11. Treasury Use

Treasury resources may be used for purposes that strengthen the long-term operation and independence of the RIGOR network.

Examples include:

### Infrastructure

* seed nodes;
* full nodes;
* network monitoring;
* backup infrastructure;
* block explorers;
* public infrastructure;
* domain and hosting costs;
* security infrastructure;
* archival services.

### Security

* independent security audits;
* consensus audits;
* cryptographic reviews;
* vulnerability research;
* incident response;
* security tooling.

### Development

* protocol development;
* wallet development;
* mining software;
* FPGA research;
* developer grants;
* SDKs;
* libraries;
* testing infrastructure;
* documentation.

### Ecosystem

* independent tools;
* payment integrations;
* wallet integrations;
* mining infrastructure;
* developer programs;
* educational materials;
* interoperability work.

Treasury expenditure should be evaluated primarily by its contribution to the resilience, usability, security, decentralization, and long-term independence of the RIGOR network.

---

# 12. Treasury and ORU Purchases

The project does not establish a permanent ORU buyback program.

Treasury funds will not be routinely converted into ORU for the purpose of supporting market price.

If the project ever acquires ORU with treasury resources for a legitimate non-price-supporting purpose, the project should disclose:

* the purpose;
* the amount;
* the acquisition method where reasonably practical;
* the relevant treasury address or transaction information where applicable; and
* the intended treatment of the acquired assets.

Any future change from this policy should be publicly disclosed before implementation whenever reasonably practical.

---

# 13. Treasury Sales

Treasury assets may be sold or converted when reasonably necessary to fund legitimate project operations.

Examples include:

* infrastructure expenses;
* security expenses;
* developer compensation;
* grants;
* legal and compliance expenses;
* operational expenses;
* emergency response;
* research and development.

Treasury sales should not be represented as market signals.

Where practical, significant treasury sales should be disclosed after execution or according to a publicly documented reporting schedule.

The project will not intentionally coordinate treasury sales with promotional campaigns designed to create buying demand for the purpose of providing an exit for treasury-controlled assets.

---

# 14. Treasury Transparency

The project intends to maintain public visibility into material treasury-controlled assets.

Where technically and operationally appropriate, the project may publish:

* treasury addresses;
* asset balances;
* significant transfers;
* treasury reports;
* major expenditures;
* grants;
* infrastructure expenditures; and
* material changes in treasury holdings.

Privacy and operational security may require certain information to be disclosed after a transaction rather than before it.

The purpose of treasury transparency is to allow independent observers to understand how project-controlled resources are being used.

---

# 15. Separation Between Project Infrastructure and Market Activity

Project infrastructure should not be used as a mechanism for controlling market activity.

In particular:

* project nodes should not censor legitimate transactions for price-related reasons;
* project mining infrastructure should not be used to manipulate market conditions;
* project websites should not display misleading market statistics;
* project-controlled social accounts should not coordinate trading campaigns;
* project-controlled services should not manufacture artificial trading activity.

RIGOR infrastructure exists to support the network, not to control its economic outcome.

---

# 16. Mining Neutrality

The RIGOR project recognizes mining as an essential component of the proof-of-work network.

The project may operate mining hardware for:

* protocol testing;
* network testing;
* hardware research;
* miner development;
* security research;
* benchmarking;
* operational research.

However, the project does not intend to become the permanent dominant source of network hashrate.

Where practical, the project should encourage:

* independent miners;
* independent mining pools;
* independent FPGA development;
* multiple hardware implementations;
* geographically distributed infrastructure; and
* open mining software.

The objective is not merely to increase total hashrate.

The objective is to encourage a broad and independent mining ecosystem.

---

# 17. Decentralization as a Treasury Objective

Treasury spending should be evaluated not only by the amount of infrastructure it creates, but also by whether it increases or decreases dependence on the project itself.

Where two technically comparable approaches are available, preference should generally be given to approaches that:

* reduce single points of failure;
* enable independent operation;
* increase geographic diversity;
* increase implementation diversity;
* reduce dependence on project-controlled infrastructure;
* encourage independent operators; and
* improve the ability of the network to continue operating without the project team.

A larger project-controlled infrastructure footprint is not automatically considered better.

---

# 18. No Exclusive Economic Control

The RIGOR project will not seek to become the exclusive:

* buyer of ORU;
* seller of ORU;
* mining pool;
* liquidity provider;
* exchange;
* wallet provider;
* node operator; or
* infrastructure provider.

Third-party participation is an intended characteristic of the ecosystem.

The long-term objective is for RIGOR to remain useful even if the original project team becomes smaller or eventually ceases active development.

---

# 19. Emergency Situations

This policy does not prevent the project from responding to genuine technical, security, or operational emergencies.

Examples may include:

* a critical consensus vulnerability;
* a severe network attack;
* a cryptographic vulnerability;
* a catastrophic infrastructure failure;
* a critical wallet vulnerability;
* a chain split;
* a severe software defect.

Emergency action should be limited to what is reasonably necessary to protect the network and its users.

Emergency authority must not be interpreted as general authority to manage ORU or RES market prices.

If emergency action materially affects treasury assets or market infrastructure, the project should disclose the circumstances and actions taken as soon as reasonably practical.

---

# 20. Policy Changes

This policy is intended to constrain the conduct of the RIGOR project team.

Changes should not be made retroactively for the purpose of legitimizing conduct that would previously have been inconsistent with this policy.

Any material amendment should:

1. be committed to the public repository;
2. identify the previous policy version;
3. identify the new policy version;
4. explain the reason for the change; and
5. include the effective date.

Changes affecting fundamental treasury or market-neutrality principles should receive public notice before becoming effective whenever reasonably practical.

---

# 21. Public Accountability

The RIGOR project recognizes that credibility cannot be created solely through statements.

Credibility is established by observable behavior over time.

Accordingly, this policy is published publicly so that users, miners, developers, investors, exchanges, and other independent participants can compare the project's future conduct against the principles stated here.

The project welcomes independent scrutiny of:

* treasury transactions;
* mining activity;
* infrastructure;
* development activity;
* public statements; and
* adherence to this policy.

If project behavior materially conflicts with this policy, the community should be able to identify and discuss that conflict using publicly verifiable information.

---

# 22. What This Policy Does Not Mean

Market neutrality does not mean that the RIGOR project is passive.

The project may actively:

* develop the protocol;
* fix bugs;
* improve security;
* operate infrastructure;
* publish software;
* support miners;
* develop FPGA implementations;
* fund independent development;
* communicate technical progress;
* pursue exchange integrations;
* publish documentation;
* improve wallets;
* expand network infrastructure; and
* encourage legitimate adoption.

The distinction is simple:

> **The project may work to make RIGOR more useful. It will not make maintaining a particular market price its objective.**

---

# 23. Guiding Principle

RIGOR's long-term value should come from the network itself:

* useful software;
* reliable consensus;
* independent verification;
* proof-of-work security;
* distributed mining;
* independent nodes;
* open development;
* real users;
* real transactions;
* real infrastructure; and
* voluntary market participation.

The project team cannot and should not determine what the market ultimately considers RIGOR to be worth.

That decision belongs to the market.

The project's responsibility is to build a network worthy of independent participation.

---

## 24. Final Commitment

The RIGOR project adopts the following commitment from mainnet genesis onward:

> **We will build and maintain the network.**
>
> **We will protect the protocol and its infrastructure.**
>
> **We will disclose material treasury activity as transparently as reasonably possible.**
>
> **We will not make ORU or RES price support a project objective.**
>
> **We will not coordinate artificial market demand, wash trading, or pump-and-dump activity.**
>
> **We will not promise investors a return or a future market price.**
>
> **We will encourage independent miners, developers, node operators, exchanges, and users.**
>
> **We will allow the market to determine the economic value of RIGOR.**
>
> **The protocol belongs to the network, not to the price.**

---

**Effective at RIGOR Mainnet Genesis.**

**Policy Version 1.0**
